Delivery Settings

Configure delivery charges for customers at checkout. Changes take effect immediately for new orders.

Standard delivery becomes free when order subtotal reaches this amount. Set to 0 to disable.
How it works
  • Customer selects Dhaka division → Inside Dhaka charge applies.
  • Any other division → Outside Dhaka charge applies.
  • Customer selects Express Delivery → Express charge always applies (no free threshold).
  • Subtotal ≥ Free Delivery Threshold → Standard delivery becomes FREE.
